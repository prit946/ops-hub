📝 What is this?
A fast, mobile-first Google Tasks app built into PritOS. Works as a PWA — add it to your home screen and it opens like a native app. Your task lists sync with Google Tasks, so anything you add here shows up in Google Tasks everywhere else too.
✨ AI Assist — What it does
Tap AI Assist at the bottom to open the AI panel. It reads your current task list and can:
Prioritize — ranks what matters most right now
Focus — picks one thing to do next
Overdue — flags what's past due
Skip — suggests low-value tasks to drop
Group — clusters similar tasks together
Game plan — builds today's schedule
You can also type a custom request like "add buy milk for tomorrow" or "delete all completed tasks" — it takes action directly on your list.
⚡ Powered by Gemini via the PritOS gateway. Requires internet.
